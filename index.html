Hello! I am Pengxiang Lan (兰鹏翔), a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Northeastern University. I am advised by Prof. Hai Zhao and Prof. Guibing Guo.

My research lies at the intersection of recommender systems, large language models, and spatio-temporal intelligence. I focus on next POI / next location recommendation, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, knowledge editing, generative recommendation, and test-time adaptation.

My work has appeared in SIGIR, ACL, AAAI, NAACL, WWW, PAKDD, DASFAA, ICWS, Expert Systems with Applications, Intelligent Data Analysis, and the Journal of Intelligent & Fuzzy Systems. For the latest citation counts and publication versions, please see my Google Scholar profile.

Project Lead · 2024.07 – 2025.07

Project Lead · 2021.05 – 2022.04

Led next-POI recommendation modeling with multi-source spatio-temporal context, combining Mogrifier LSTM, multi-head self-attention, location-salient and time-weighting mechanisms, and geographic non-local modeling for dynamic preference learning.
